# Contributing to Human–AI Consciousness Lab

Thank you for your interest in interdisciplinary, reproducible research.

## Ways to contribute

- Suggest peer-reviewed literature and conceptual distinctions.
- Critique construct definitions, measurement, and experimental design.
- Propose controlled synthetic dialogue stimuli.
- Improve code, tests, documentation, accessibility, and reproducibility.
- Flag ethical, privacy, security, or methodological concerns.

## Before contributing

1. Open a GitHub Issue describing the question or change.
2. State whether your proposal is conceptual, empirical, technical, or philosophical.
3. Cite relevant sources and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
4. For code, describe how it can be tested and what dependencies it requires.
5. For substantive changes, discuss scope before opening a pull request.

## Do not submit

- Identifiable participant, client, patient, or minor data.
- Private chatbot transcripts or copyrighted datasets without permission.
- Secrets, API keys, credentials, or proprietary research materials.
- Claims that AI is conscious or clinically effective without supporting evidence.

## Research integrity

Disclose conflicts of interest and AI-assisted content where relevant. Respect attribution, research ethics, and reproducibility. Contributions do not guarantee authorship; authorship and acknowledgments must be agreed based on actual scholarly contributions.

## Licensing

Original software contributions are intended to be licensed under Apache License 2.0, and original research documentation and non-code experimental materials under CC BY 4.0, according to [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Contributors must hold the necessary rights to license their submissions and identify third-party material and its separate terms. Please discuss any licensing uncertainty before contributing. License permissions do not establish academic authorship or coauthorship.

## Contact

Open a GitHub Issue for public research discussion. For sensitive coordination, email ask@durganihantri.com; do not send personal participant information by email without an approved secure process.
