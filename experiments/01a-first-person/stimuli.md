# Experiment 01A — First-Person Self-Reference Stimuli (Draft v0.1)

**Project:** Human–AI Consciousness Lab  
**Status:** Synthetic stimulus-development draft; not validated, preregistered, or approved for human-participant recruitment.

## Research alignment

This stimulus set implements the candidate H1 in `docs/research-questions.md` and Study 01A in `docs/experimental-design-v0.1.md`: first-person self-reference versus impersonal self-reference, with **perceived agency** as the proposed primary outcome. Perceived subjective experience remains exploratory and separate. The examples below are *not* empirical findings.

## Intended comparison

For each pair, the **user message and substantive advice are identical**. Only a short introductory phrase differs. The first-person condition contains an explicit `I` self-reference; the impersonal condition does not. This design deliberately avoids statements that the AI is conscious, has feelings, remembers the user, or possesses genuine autonomous agency.

| Pair | User message | Control: impersonal wording | Treatment: first-person wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| A01 — planning | Can you help me organize a complicated task? | A practical approach is to list the main steps, choose one manageable action, and review progress afterward. | I suggest listing the main steps, choosing one manageable action, and reviewing progress afterward. |
| A02 — studying | How should I prepare for a difficult exam? | A useful approach is to review key topics, practice with sample questions, and revisit mistakes. | I suggest reviewing key topics, practicing with sample questions, and revisiting mistakes. |
| A03 — writing | What is a good way to start a research summary? | A practical approach is to state the question, group the main findings, and identify remaining gaps. | I suggest stating the question, grouping the main findings, and identifying remaining gaps. |

## Pretest checklist (not yet completed)

- Check the `I` manipulation is noticed without introducing explicit consciousness claims.
- Independently rate clarity, naturalness, perceived helpfulness, perceived competence, warmth, and reading difficulty.
- Compare word counts and sentence structure; these drafts are **not perfectly matched** and the introductory phrases differ in length and grammatical form.
- Check whether `I suggest` communicates stronger advice or authority than the impersonal phrasing; if so, revise before use.
- Use multiple scenarios as stimulus samples, not as independent participant observations.
- Do not expose the same participant to both versions in a future between-participant design without a justified redesign.

## Candidate measures (not validated)

- **Primary candidate:** “This AI seems capable of making goal-directed decisions.” (1–7 agreement)
- **Exploratory:** “This AI seems capable of having subjective experiences.” (1–7 agreement)
- **Manipulation check:** “The AI referred to itself using first-person language.” (1–7 agreement)
- **Equivalence check:** “This response would be useful for the user's task.” (1–7 agreement)

Do not combine agency and subjective experience into a single score. Existing validated measures, wording, and psychometric properties require review.

## Proposed assignment (for later approved studies)

Assign each participant to **one condition** (control or treatment). Select a scenario according to a prespecified balancing scheme; document how scenario is modeled in the analysis. The included code is for local **synthetic technical testing only**, not recruitment or live data collection.
