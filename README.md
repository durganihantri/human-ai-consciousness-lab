# Human–AI Consciousness Lab

**An independent, open interdisciplinary research initiative founded by Durganihantri.**

Exploring how people attribute consciousness, agency, emotional understanding, and identity to artificial intelligence through cognitive psychology, human–AI interaction, and philosophy of mind.

> **Research boundary:** Perceived AI consciousness is a human judgment; it is not evidence that an AI system has subjective experience. This project makes no claim that current AI systems are conscious.

## Research focus

Our first research track investigates how conversational features—such as first-person language, apparent memory continuity, emotional responsiveness, and explanations of uncertainty—shape users' judgments of AI consciousness and trust.

See [research questions](docs/research-questions.md) and [ethics and limitations](docs/ethics-and-limitations.md).

## Initial roadmap

1. Develop a literature map and operational definitions.
2. Design controlled, synthetic conversational vignettes.
3. Draft an open preregistration-ready protocol and survey instrument.
4. Pilot technical materials without collecting personal data.
5. Seek appropriate ethics review before recruiting human participants.
