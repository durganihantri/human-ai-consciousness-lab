# Ethics and Limitations (Draft v0.1)

## What this project studies

We study **people's perceptions and attributions** regarding AI. Ratings of perceived consciousness do not establish that AI has subjective experience, moral status, or independent agency.

## Human participants

No recruitment, surveys, interviews, or collection of identifiable interaction logs should begin without an appropriate ethics review and a documented consent and data-management plan. Requirements vary by institution and jurisdiction; collaborators must determine applicable approvals before starting.

## Privacy and data minimization

Use synthetic dialogue examples and synthetic test data during initial development. Do not commit identifiable transcripts, client records, children's data, raw participant responses, or credentials to public repositories. Define retention, access, withdrawal, deletion, and publication practices before data collection.

## Potential risks

- Anthropomorphic prompts may influence participants' beliefs or emotional attachment.
- Measures of empathy, agency, and consciousness may overlap conceptually.
- Cultural and linguistic differences may limit generalizability.
- AI outputs and model versions can change, affecting reproducibility.
- Self-report measures do not directly measure subjective experience in AI.

## Safeguards

Use transparent descriptions of study aims, debriefing when appropriate, non-deceptive procedures where possible, balanced stimuli, preregistration for confirmatory tests, and clear reporting of uncertainty and null results.

## No clinical claims

This initiative does not provide diagnosis, therapy, or validated psychological assessment tools. Research materials must not be presented as medical or clinical advice.

## Open science boundaries

Share protocols, code, synthetic stimuli, and appropriately de-identified aggregate results where lawful and ethically appropriate. Never assume that making a repository public authorizes release of participant data or third-party materials.

## Review status

This is an initial ethics planning document, **not an ethics approval** or institutional endorsement.
