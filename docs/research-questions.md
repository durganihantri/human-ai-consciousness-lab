# Research Questions and Initial Hypotheses (Draft v0.1)

## Scope

This track examines **human attributions** of consciousness to AI, not whether AI actually experiences consciousness.

## Primary research question

**RQ1:** How do conversational cues influence users' perceived AI consciousness?

## Secondary research questions

- **RQ2:** How does first-person self-reference affect perceived agency and subjective experience?
- **RQ3:** How does apparent conversational continuity affect perceived identity and trust?
- **RQ4:** Does emotionally responsive language increase perceived understanding, and is that effect separable from perceived consciousness?
- **RQ5:** How do prior beliefs about AI and AI familiarity moderate these judgments?

## Candidate hypotheses — not preregistered

- **H1:** Vignettes with first-person self-reference will yield higher mean perceived-agency ratings than otherwise matched neutral vignettes.
- **H2:** Vignettes describing continuity across conversations will yield higher perceived-identity ratings than matched discontinuous vignettes.
- **H3:** Empathetic wording will raise perceived-emotional-understanding ratings; its effect on perceived subjective experience remains exploratory.

These are proposals, not findings. Hypotheses, exclusion criteria, measures, and analysis plans must be fixed before confirmatory data collection.

## Candidate study design

Use synthetic, controlled chatbot dialogue vignettes that vary one feature at a time. Randomize presentation order. Collect separate ratings of perceived consciousness, agency, emotional understanding, identity continuity, and trust; avoid collapsing them into a single construct without validation.

## Measurement and validity questions

- What does each construct mean operationally?
- Are existing scales appropriate, licensed, and psychometrically supported for this context?
- How do we separate anthropomorphism, likability, and perceived consciousness?
- What sample, power analysis, cultural/language scope, and exclusion rules are appropriate?
- How will materials and analysis code be reproducible without exposing participant data?

## Immediate deliverables

1. Literature matrix with citations and competing theoretical perspectives.
2. Operational definition table.
3. Synthetic vignette set and manipulation checks.
4. Preregistration draft and analysis plan.
5. Ethics review and participant information materials, if human research is pursued.
